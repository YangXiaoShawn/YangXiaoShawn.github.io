Research question

Do equity microstructure factors travel to crypto?

Crypto offers open minute-bar and perpetual-futures data. I test whether ideas from an A-share microstructure research series carry over to this different market.

01 · Method

I test cross-sectional factors on 105 coins, check split samples and parameters, compare funding on OKX, and track the composite after publication.

02 · Conclusion

Most factors do not travel; funding and positioning hold up better.

Funding (+1.42 Sharpe), retail positioning (+1.39), and a low-price range factor (+1.63) support a market-neutral composite in the 730-day panel. Post-publication windows include both gains and losses.

Scope of the evidence. Backtest and out-of-sample backtest association, not live trading. Next-close fills omit market impact; the sample is an altcoin bear market and the OKX check is short.

Research question and cross-market transfer

Question: Do the factors of a 32-report sell-side market-microstructure series written for China A-shares carry over to crypto, where minute bars, taker-side order flow, and perpetual-futures data are freely available?

Short 120-day crypto windows easily manufacture apparent Sharpe > 3.0 factors that turn negative over a full 730-day cycle. Extending the panel to 105 coins × 730 days (2024-06 to 2026-05) shows that most equity order-flow and momentum factors fail or prove parameter-fragile; the signals that survive are perpetual-futures funding rates, retail long/short positioning, and low-price range amplitude.

Capabilities demonstrated

  1. Reconstruct 32 equity microstructure and derivatives factors from 1-minute spot/perpetual klines, funding rates, open interest, and long/short ratios.
  2. Enforce split-sample sign consistency, parameter-grid stability, rolling-window positivity, universe-breadth checks, and cross-exchange replication on OKX.
  3. Decompose funding-rate factor returns into price reversal (83%) versus direct funding carry accrual (17%).
  4. Freeze the deliverable market-neutral composite at publication (2026-05-31 data cutoff) and report unmodified out-of-sample monthly updates for June, July, August, and September 2026 without re-fitting.
