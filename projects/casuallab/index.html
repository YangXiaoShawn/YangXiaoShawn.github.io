Research question

Who should get the offer?

A discount can increase activity. The harder question is whether a model can identify who will respond—and spend the same budget better.

01 · Method

I test effect estimates against known answers, then compare spending rules at the same budget in simulated markets held out from training.

02 · Conclusion

The complex targeting model has not earned its advantage.

The learner misses the recovery benchmark. In the budget experiment, a uniform allocation also produces more incremental trips than the model-based rule.

Scope of the evidence. Semi-synthetic validation, not a measured effect of a real NYC promotion. The oracle knows the true average effect; it is a test benchmark, not a deployable policy.

Research question and relevance

Question: How do causal mechanisms and heterogeneous treatment effects shape market outcomes and policy interventions in field-like datasets?

Credible policy evaluation requires more than a point estimate. The project connects identification assumptions, estimator recovery, heterogeneous effects, intervention allocation, and reporting so a decision can be traced back to evidence.

Original paper or report: CasualLab is a project-originated research toolkit; this release does not claim to reproduce a specific third-party paper without a documented citation and benchmark.
