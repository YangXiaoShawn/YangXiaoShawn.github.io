Layer 01 · Shared Engine

oeq-core v0.2.0 Infrastructure

Centralized statistical and portfolio primitives imported across subprojects with 100% unit-test coverage:

  • Shrinkage & Covariance: Empirical Bayes adaptive cross-sectional shrinkage & Ledoit-Wolf covariance regularization.
  • Inference & Multiple Testing: Newey-West HAC t-statistics, Politis-Romano stationary bootstrap, White Reality Check, and Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR).

Selected research

Eight questions, with the evidence in view.

Experiments & incentives

Who should get the offer?

The complex targeting model has not earned its advantage.

I test effect estimates against known answers, then compare spending rules at the same budget in simulated markets held out from training.

Read the study & evidence →
Crypto funding & positioning

Do equity microstructure factors travel to crypto?

Most factors do not travel; funding and positioning hold up better.

I test cross-sectional factors on 105 coins, check split samples and parameters, compare funding on OKX, and track the composite after publication.

Read the study & evidence →
Emerging-market factor timing

Can machine learning time equity factors?

Pooling explains much of the apparent predictability.

I compare out-of-sample linear, tree, and neural-network forecasts on JKP global factors, test developed-to-emerging-market transfer, and evaluate timing after turnover costs.

Read the study & evidence →
Forecasting & information

Would the forecast work in real time?

Changing the data vintage changes which models look best.

I reconstruct official release histories and rerun forecasts with three information rules: data known then, revised values with the same availability, and unrestricted latest values.

Read the study & evidence →
Market microstructure

How much short-horizon edge does order flow carry?

Order flow carries a positive gross edge in 110 of 144 execution scenarios.

I replay one four-hour BTC/ETH order-book capture across two evaluation phases, four horizons, and nine delay combinations, with a fixed 4 bp fee.

Read the study & evidence →
Polymarket & prediction markets

Which prediction-market signals survive a realistic backtest?

Reversal, volatility, and illiquidity survive the corrections.

I study 4,000 Polymarket markets, remove five backtest artifacts, control for price levels, and evaluate factor signs on a held-out half of the panel.

Read the study & evidence →
Housing & household finance

Does a cheap mortgage keep a loan in place?

Bigger rate gaps are associated with fewer mortgage exits.

I compare monthly payoff rates across rate gaps, then estimate a complementary log-log hazard model with observed loan and local-market controls.

Trade & public policy

Who absorbed the tariff?

The evidence points toward higher costs for U.S. importers.

I compare each wave of tariffed products with never-tariffed products, tracking unit import values before and after the policy and checking three sample windows.
