Experiments & incentives

Who should get the offer?

The complex targeting model has not earned its advantage.

I test effect estimates against known answers, then compare spending rules at the same budget in simulated markets held out from training.

Forecasting & information

Would the forecast work in real time?

Changing the data vintage changes which models look best.

I reconstruct official release histories and rerun forecasts with three information rules: data known then, revised values with the same availability, and unrestricted latest values.

Market microstructure

Can a trading signal survive its costs?

After fees, none of the 144 scenarios remains positive.

I replay one four-hour BTC/ETH order-book capture across two evaluation phases, four horizons, and nine delay combinations, with a fixed 4 bp fee.

Housing & household finance

Does a cheap mortgage keep a loan in place?

Bigger rate gaps are associated with fewer mortgage exits.

I compare monthly payoff rates across rate gaps, then estimate a complementary log-log hazard model with observed loan and local-market controls.

Who absorbed the tariff?

The evidence points toward higher costs for U.S. importers.

I compare each wave of tariffed products with never-tariffed products, tracking unit import values before and after the policy and checking three sample windows.
