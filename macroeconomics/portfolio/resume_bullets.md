# Resume Bullets

- Built a Python 3.12 real-time macro nowcasting engine using Polars, Parquet, DuckDB, scikit-learn, and statsmodels, with configurable payroll, core-inflation, and real-GDP targets across quarterly, monthly, weekly, and daily data.
- Designed strict vintage-leakage controls that propagate source availability through transformed features and automatically reject post-origin inputs.
- Implemented expanding-window benchmark and regularized/tree model evaluation with fold-local preprocessing, residual intervals, revision analysis, and guarded forecast-comparison tests.
- Automated reproducibility manifests, exact linear news attribution, one-page policy briefs, multi-target research reports, and a target-selectable local monitoring dashboard.
- Separated synthetic validation from empirical claims; distinguished current-data BLS/BEA APIs from historical archives; and gated live ingestion behind source-specific terms, credential-redaction, and coverage audits.
- Acquired, hashed, and parsed 626,304 official archive rows across 21 BLS, BEA, DOL,
  Federal Reserve, Treasury, and Census series; reproduced 15,264 two-horizon real-data
  forecasts with zero strict information-timing violations while retaining documented
  archive and publication-timing limitations.
