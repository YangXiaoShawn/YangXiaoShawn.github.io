Questions first. Evidence throughout.

My work connects applied economics with quantitative research: who responds to an incentive, how data revisions affect a forecast, why mortgage loans stay in place, who bears a tariff, and whether a trading signal survives its costs.

I build the work from the source data onward: define the question, choose the comparison, implement the analysis, test alternatives, and make the findings inspectable. A useful result can be a positive finding, a failed benchmark, or a limit on what the data can establish.

How to read this portfolio

Each study starts with its central question and a plain-language conclusion. The figures show the supporting results; the notes explain the research design, assumptions, and remaining uncertainty. Exact values and published sources sit beside the charts.

The website is a guided introduction to the research. The interactive Space lets you compare metrics, inspect simulation scenarios, and browse the public evidence. Source repositories contain the methods and reproduction routes.
